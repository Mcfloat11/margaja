1.5 CURRENT WORKFLOW/PROCESS

The current workflow of Shadrack Talabahan relies heavily on manual paper-based methods:

  1. Customer (dine-in or retail) orders seafood products
  2. Staff manually checks cold storage and display areas for availability
  3. Staff looks up prices from memorized values or cheat sheets
  4. Transaction is hand-written into a physical logbook
  5. Customer pays and receives hand-written receipt
  6. At end of day, owner manually calculates total sales using calculator
  7. Stock counts are done manually on a weekly basis

1.6 PROBLEMS AND CHALLENGES ENCOUNTERED

a) Stock Management Issues:

  • Manual counting leads to frequent miscounts
  • No real-time inventory tracking
  • Difficulty in identifying low stock items

b) Sales Recording Issues:

  • Illegible handwriting leads to lost data
  • Misplaced receipts and logbooks
  • No backup of sales records

c) Pricing Issues:

  • Inconsistent pricing due to memorized values
  • Difficulty updating prices when supplier rates change
  • No profit margin tracking

d) Reporting Issues:

  • Manual calculation of daily/weekly sales is time-consuming
  • Prone to mathematical errors
  • No proper sales analytics

2. SYSTEM PROPOSAL

2.1 PROJECT TITLE

Shadrack Talabahan: Seafood Restaurant and Retail System

2.2 PROJECT OBJECTIVES

General Objective:
To develop a web-based inventory and sales management system for Shadrack Talabahan that will streamline business operations, improve accuracy, and provide better data management for both restaurant and retail seafood operations.

Specific Objectives:

  1. To automate inventory tracking of fresh seafood products and reduce manual counting errors
  2. To provide an efficient Point of Sale (POS) interface for both restaurant and retail sales transactions
  3. To maintain accurate records of sales, customers, and seafood products
  4. To generate reports for business analysis and decision-making
  5. To implement user roles and access control for system security

2.3 PROPOSED SYSTEM DESCRIPTION

Shadrack Talabahan Seafood Restaurant and Retail System will enable store owners and staff to easily organize inventory tracking of fresh marine products and manage financial sales records, guaranteeing optimal use of available resources and reducing manual counting errors. Customers can conveniently make purchases by visiting the physical restaurant or retail shop, and all transaction data is securely stored in the system.

The database stores details about seafood inventory items, including stock balances, wholesale buying prices, retail selling prices, and automated profit margins, enabling effective product management. The system allows seamless processing and recording of sales based on real-time price rules, improving transaction accuracy and service speed. Additionally, the system integrates a reporting feature that allows users to instantly export performance reports for daily or weekly auditing.

Overall, the Seafood Restaurant and Retail System offers a dependable and user-friendly platform for both business owners and staff, improving operational effectiveness, recording accuracy, and business performance.

3.2 ENTITY RELATIONSHIPS

  1. USER (1) → (M) SALE
    - One user can process many sales
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: SALES.cashier_user_id → USERS.id
  2. USER (1) → (M) SALE (Voided By)
    - One user can void many sales
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: SALES.voided_by → USERS.id
  3. USER (1) → (M) TBL_LOGS
    - One user can have many activity logs
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: TBL_LOGS.USERID → USERS.id
  4. USER (1) → (M) STOCK_MOVEMENTS
    - One user can initiate many stock movements
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: STOCK_MOVEMENTS.user_id → USERS.id
  5. CATEGORY (1) → (M) PRODUCTS
    - One category can have many products
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: PRODUCTS.category_id → CATEGORIES.id
  6. PRODUCTS (1) → (M) SALE_ITEMS
    - One product can appear in many sale items
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: SALE_ITEMS.product_id → PRODUCTS.id
  7. PRODUCTS (1) → (M) STOCK_MOVEMENTS
    - One product can have many stock movements
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: STOCK_MOVEMENTS.product_id → PRODUCTS.id
  8. SALES (1) → (M) SALE_ITEMS
    - One sale can have many sale items
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: SALE_ITEMS.sale_id → SALES.id
  9. USER (1) → (M) LOGIN_ATTEMPTS
    - One user can have many login attempts
    - Relationship: One-to-Many
    - Foreign Key: LOGIN_ATTEMPTS.user_id → USERS.id
